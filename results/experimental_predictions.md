## 5. What would falsify the cc̄+bb̄-determined description

* A 1P centroid outside 6745–6768 MeV, or a ³P₂–³P₀ splitting outside 46–82 MeV: no potential in the scanned family, fitted to cc̄+bb̄, produces it.
* A B_c*(2S)–B_c(2S) splitting below 30 MeV or above 40 MeV (currently 35.4 ± 2.4 from ATLAS+CMS).
* A 3³S₁ below 7253 MeV or above 7335 MeV.
* A 1D multiplet whose centroid lies more than 30 MeV from 7035 MeV, or with the ³D₁ above the ³D₃.
* Any B_c excited state observed below 6690 MeV.
